AirAware: assistant redesign wireframes

Low-fidelity layouts for owner review, Phase 1. Text in [brackets] is live data filled by the app; no real readings are shown here. Card titles and texts are the real rule cards from rules.ts. Spec: 2026-11-20-assistant-redesign-design.md.

1. Today: one situation, one action

The verdict is the largest text. One recommendation shows without a button. Everything technical folds into "Air quality details". The bottom bar (phone) and top bar (desktop) appear on every app page.

2. Air quality details and Today's states

Details hold every technical part, unchanged. States keep the verdict honest: stale is never hidden, and missing data says so.

3. Onboarding: three short, skippable steps

Shown on a first visit or when no place is chosen. Steps 2 and 3 can be skipped. Nothing is saved unless "Remember on this device" is ticked. On desktop, the same steps sit in a centred 560 px column.

4. Ask AirAware: guided questions, answers from the rules

Questions are matched to the existing advice cards and today's forecast. AI only writes the 2–3 sentence answer, under the same checks as the daily plan. Never yes or no on safety. Nothing typed here is saved or logged.

5. My Home and Better Habits

Today's cards for you first, then evergreen advice grouped by topic. "Why it matters" opens the same reason, sources and caveat as "Why?". Groups marked new need new cards with cited sources (D4), shown to you before they ship.

6. Landing: eight sections

Benefits before plumbing. Two buttons in the hero, one at the end. Screenshots are real captures of the new app, labelled as past screenshots with their date. Built after Today, so the screenshots show the new design.
