Sustainable everyday choices

Practical habits that cut pollution for you and your neighbours. We never show an emissions saving we can't calculate.

Combine trips and avoid idling

Combining errands into one trip and switching off the engine while waiting cut your vehicle's exhaust. For some trips, public transport, carpooling, walking or cycling may also be practical.

How AirAware works

Forecasts for the next 48 hours, hour by hour.

  1. CAMS global model

    Copernicus runs it every 12 hours on a grid about 45 km wide.

  2. Open-Meteo

    Serves its hourly PM2.5, PM10 and four gases, each with a unit.

  3. AirAware server

    Asks only for a location rounded to about 1 km and reuses each answer for 10 minutes.

  4. Your screen

    Every value with its unit, its hour and the label Model forecast.

Forecasts are model estimates, not station measurements, and not the CPCB National AQI.

Official CPCB observations are not connected yet.
