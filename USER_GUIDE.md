# AirAware AI — User Guide

## 1. Choose a location
Search for a city or area. You can use manual selection without sharing GPS. If you choose browser geolocation, the browser will ask for permission.

## 2. Understand the data card
Check:
- Provider/source.
- Observation or forecast label.
- Date and time.
- Pollutant and unit.
- Station or model-grid information, if available.
- Fresh/stale/unknown status.

A forecast is not a station measurement. A pollutant concentration is not automatically the overall CPCB National AQI.

## 3. Set optional preferences
Choose only the context you want to share:
- Commute mode.
- Outdoor exercise habit.
- Cooking/ventilation context.
- Optional household preferences.
- Preferred language.

You can skip optional fields. Do not enter diagnoses or medical records.

## 4. Generate a daily plan
Review suggested actions by time of day. Each action should explain:
- What to do.
- Why it applies.
- Which data/preferences influenced it.
- Source or rule type.
- Assumptions and limitations.

## 5. Improve indoor air
Review source-control and ventilation guidance in the context of both indoor sources and outdoor pollution. Do not assume closing or opening windows is always correct.

## 6. Explore sustainable swaps
Choose practical habit changes that suit your life. Treat qualitative benefits as suggestions; do not assume a numeric emissions reduction unless a documented calculation is displayed.

## 7. Manage saved data
Save plans only when useful. Use settings to change preferences and remove saved data where those controls are available.

## 8. Important limitation
AirAware AI provides general information, not medical diagnosis or treatment. It cannot guarantee an activity or place is safe. Follow local public-health advisories and consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical concerns.
